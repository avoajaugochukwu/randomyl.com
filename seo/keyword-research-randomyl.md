## What to do with this (analyst read)

- **QR codes is the giant** (~4.2M searches/mo) but the head term `qr code generator` is MEDIUM–HIGH comp and owned by Canva/Adobe/QRCode Monkey. Win on **long-tail + use-case** (`qr code generator online`, `free qr code generator online`, industry/format variants) and a fast, free, no-signup tool angle.
- **Phone numbers is almost entirely LOW competition** — the strongest cluster to actually rank in. Note intent split: much of the volume is *receive-SMS / temp-number / numbers-to-call*, not just "generate a fake number." Consider whether the tool/content addresses verification & testing use-cases.
- **Bible verses** carries a surprisingly high CPC (~$9.71) and solid volume — `random bible verse`, `random verse in bible`, daily-verse and topical variants are content+tool plays.
- **Random words/nouns & objects** are LOW comp, decent volume, near-zero CPC (low monetization but cheap, easy traffic) — good for programmatic/long-tail tool pages.
- Rows sharing identical volume are Google **close-variants** (collapsed here); brand terms (Canva, Adobe, QRCode Monkey) are excluded from the opportunity list since they're not realistically winnable.

## Method & caveats

- Seeds → fanout: **DataForSEO Labs** `keyword_suggestions` + `related_keywords` (depth 2) across 6 clusters; **Apify** Google Autocomplete alphabet-soup (seed + a–z) on the 5 core tools.
- Metrics (volume / CPC / competition) = Google Ads, United States, English, last-12-mo average. Volume is Google's bucketed estimate; CPC `—` means Ads reported no bid data.
- 404 harvested keywords had no measurable volume (ultra long-tail) — kept in the CSV, excluded from tables.
- Total API spend for this run: **$0.47**. Full 1,508-keyword dataset (incl. zero-volume) is in `keyword-research-randomyl.csv`.
