### What is a QR Code and How Does It Work?

A **QR code (Quick Response code)** is a type of barcode that stores information in a way that can be quickly scanned using a smartphone camera. Unlike traditional barcodes, which only hold a small amount of data, QR codes can store URLs, contact details, Wi-Fi passwords, and much more.

When you scan a QR code, your phone reads the encoded information and performs an action—like opening a website, adding a contact, or even connecting to Wi-Fi automatically. This makes them a powerful tool for both personal and business use.

### Are QR Codes Safe to Use?

QR codes are generally safe, but like any technology, they can be misused. Here are some quick tips to stay safe:

- **Only scan QR codes from trusted sources.**
- **Use a QR code scanner with a preview feature** so you can see the URL before visiting.
- **Avoid scanning random QR codes in public places**—they could lead to phishing websites.
- **Use a dynamic QR code generator** that allows you to edit the destination later, in case you need to change the link.
