### What is a QR Code and Why is it Useful?

A **QR code** is a type of barcode that can store information like website links, phone numbers, or even text. When you scan it with your smartphone’s camera, it instantly takes you to the stored information—no typing required!

QR codes are popular because they:

- **Save time** – No need to type long URLs or search for information manually.
- **Are contactless** – Perfect for menus, event tickets, and payments.
- **Can store a variety of data** – Websites, PDFs, email addresses, WiFi passwords, and more.
- **Enhance engagement** – Great for marketing campaigns and social media profiles.

Businesses, schools, and even individuals are using QR codes to make life easier and more interactive.

### Best Free QR Code Makers (Comparison)

Not all free QR code generators are created equal. Here’s a quick comparison of some popular options:

- **Static QR Codes**: The content is permanent and cannot be changed.
- **Dynamic QR Codes**: You can edit the content without needing to generate a new code.

If you plan to update your QR code’s content often, a **dynamic QR code** (usually available with paid plans) is a better choice.

### Are Free QR Code Makers Safe?

Yes, most free QR code generators are safe, but be cautious. Here are some tips:

- **Use reputable QR code makers** – Stick with well-known platforms to avoid malware.
- **Check privacy policies** – Some free tools may track your QR code scans.
- **Avoid entering sensitive information** – Never store personal passwords or banking details in a QR code.
